Terms of Service

The plain-language version, because there's no fine print behind it

Juke is a fantasy football analyst for your draft and your season. It runs mock drafts against a board built from real draft data and grades every one. It reads the league you actually play in and makes the week's calls on it — who to start, who to claim, whether a trade is fair — with the arithmetic printed beside each one. And it keeps every player, every team in your league and every draft you've run on pages you can check. It only ever reads a league; it never changes one.

Matched to what the app actually does today rather than copied from a template. It hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. 01Using Juke

Most of Juke needs nothing from you: open the page and run a mock draft, look up any player, or see what the week's calls look like on a sample league. There's nothing to install, no age gate, and no account needed for any of that. Reading your own league is the part that needs an account — sections 02 and 03 cover it. By using the site you're agreeing to these terms; if that's not something you're willing to do, the thing to do differently is not use it.

Don't try to abuse the service — scripting requests at it, attempting to bypass the room's rate limits, or trying to break into a room you weren't invited to. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what a room holds and for how long.

02Your account, and what costs money

An account is free to create. It does two things: it keeps the draft you're in the middle of, and the drafts you've finished, following you between browsers instead of living in one; and it holds the leagues you connect. Everything else works signed out.

Sign-in is handled by Clerk rather than by us, so their terms cover the account itself — keep your login details to yourself, and use a real address you can receive mail at, since that is how the account is recovered. Don't create accounts to get around a limit or to impersonate somebody else.

You can delete your account from Profile and security in the Account menu, and that removes what Juke stored against it as well — your drafts, the leagues you connected, and any sign-in you gave us to read one. The account and its data go together, with nothing left to ask us for. Deleting a single draft from your locker deletes it from your account too.

Nothing on Juke costs money today. Mock drafts, the player pages and the sample league are free. Reading a real league is part of a plan called Season Pass, and reading more than one is part of Multi-League; neither is on sale yet, and nobody is charged for either. When they do go on sale, the price and what it includes will be on the page before you pay. The mock draft stays free — and you won't be charged for something you are already using without being asked first.

03Connecting a league

Connecting a league lets Juke read it from the platform that runs it — Sleeper, ESPN or CBS today, with Yahoo being tested. It reads the league's teams, rosters, settings, schedule and results, and that is all it does with them. Juke never changes a league: nothing it does can set a lineup, make a claim or propose a trade. Every call is advice that you carry out yourself, on the platform, or don't.

Only connect a league you belong to, with your own details. Some platforms need more than a league's id: a private ESPN league needs your ESPN sign-in cookies, a CBS league needs your CBS cookie, and a Yahoo league needs you to approve Juke on Yahoo's own page. The Privacy Policy says exactly how each of those is stored and when it is deleted. Disconnecting a league, from Account and leagues, stops Juke reading it.

Your use of each platform is still covered by that platform's own terms. Juke isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Sleeper, ESPN, CBS or Yahoo, and a platform can change what it lets outside apps read — so a connection that works today may stop working, and Juke can't promise otherwise.

04Rooms and other people

A shared draft room is other people's picks and other people's chat messages, not just yours. Juke mechanically strips anything that isn't plain text or a real GIPHY link out of chat, and rate-limits how fast any one person can act — but it does not moderate what people say to each other beyond that. You're responsible for what you type, and for who you send an invite link to.

05Where the data comes from

Player data, projections, ADP and statistics come from public feeds: Sleeper, Fantasy Football Calculator, nflverse and ffopportunity. Player news headlines come from Tank01 and live scores from ESPN. A league you connect is read from the platform that runs it. Juke links to news rather than republishing it, and doesn't claim to have written any of it.

Every rating, grade, ranking and call on the site is Juke's own calculation on top of that data. The one exception is a platform's own weekly projection for your league, which Juke shows where the platform publishes one and labels as theirs. None of it is an endorsement from any of those providers, and none of it is expert analysis from anybody on staff — there isn't one. See how the Draft Room works for exactly how each number in a draft is computed.

06No guarantees about the football

Every grade, projection, ranking and call in Juke is a model's best guess at games that haven't been played yet. That is as true of a lineup call on your real league as of a pick in a mock draft. None of it is a promise about how any player will actually perform, and none of it is advice about money — an entry fee, a prize or a bet. What you do in your own league is your decision.

07The service as it is

Juke is provided as it stands, with no warranty that it will be available, uninterrupted or error-free. It depends on other people's servers — Sleeper, Fantasy Football Calculator, Tank01, ESPN, CBS, Yahoo, Clerk, Cloudflare — and an outage on their end can mean a feature here goes quiet: a connected league stops updating, or a call waits for the data it needs. The mock draft is built to keep working even when that happens, but that's an engineering goal, not a guarantee.
